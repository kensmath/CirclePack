CirclePack Miscellany

* Certain C/C++ calls may be made if the executables are available. Two are Shewchuk's "triangle" code for Delaunay trianulations in the plane and "QHull" for triangulations of the sphere. For circle packing in certain situations and for large packings, there are rountines from GOPack, developed by Collins, Orick, and Stephenson.

* For computers without middle or right mouse buttons:
use "button1 + ctrl" for middle and "button1 + shift" for right.

* The command "set_display -m" will attempt to optimize the size of canvasses vis-a-vis the computer screen area available.
